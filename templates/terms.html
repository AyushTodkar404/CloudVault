Terms of Service

Guidelines and governance rules for using the CloudVault document system.

Academic Year 2025–2026 • Department of Computer Engineering

5.0 GB Quota

Each account receives a 5.0 GB document quota, applied across the configured storage backend.

Clean vault policy

Automated integrity checks reject executables and malicious scripts on upload.

Auditability

Document operations are recorded in an activity log for accountability and review.

1 Acceptance of Terms

By registering or signing into CloudVault, you agree to comply with institutional academic regulations and these Terms of Service. CloudVault is provided as part of the Cloud Computing Laboratory curriculum (2025–2026).

2 Acceptable Use & Prohibited Content

Users must not upload, share, or distribute: (a) executable malware, worms, trojans, ransomware, or backdoor scripts (.exe, .bat, .cmd, .sh, .ps1); (b) copyrighted material without authorization; (c) content that violates college disciplinary or privacy guidelines. CloudVault reserves the right to reject and remove non-compliant files automatically.

3 Service Availability & Offline Mode

CloudVault leverages scalable cloud storage and web hosting architectures. Offline synchronization is supported via client-side local caching so work can proceed uninterrupted during network interruptions.
