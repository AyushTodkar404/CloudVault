Data privacy & ownership

Institutional Privacy Policy

CloudVault keeps account access and document ownership explicit, with storage protections determined by the configured deployment.

Academic Session 2025–2026 • Department of Computer Engineering

Storage protection

Cloud deployments use the configured provider's storage protection; local deployments rely on the host filesystem and access controls.

User isolation

Account-scoped access ensures only you and authorized share links can read your documents.

Zero ad trackers

No third-party trackers, marketing pixels, or selling of personal academic records.

1 Information We Collect

We collect only the bare minimum information necessary to maintain your authenticated cloud storage session: your chosen username, registered institutional email, cryptographic password hash (scrypt-derived via Werkzeug), and file metadata (file size, MIME type, upload timestamp). We do not collect browsing history or device tracking data.

2 Cloud Storage & Infrastructure Security

Files are stored using the configured storage backend. In production, deploy CloudVault behind HTTPS and apply the provider's database, object-storage, backup, and retention controls to the environment.

3 Student Data Sovereignty & Ownership

You retain full intellectual property ownership of every file uploaded to CloudVault. We do not inspect, parse, or index your private document contents for machine learning or external advertising purposes.

4 Data Portability & Account Erasure

You have the right to export your entire document and activity archive at any time via the Download Data Backup tool in Account Settings. You also have the absolute right to erasure: clicking Delete Account permanently purges your account credentials, database records, and all stored files.
